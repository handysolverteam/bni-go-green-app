Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

1. Overview

Handychapter respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal data of chapter leaders, directors, and members using our services. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you sign in using Google Single Sign-On (OAuth).

2. Information We Collect

When you authenticate via Google Sign-In, we request access only to your basic Google profile information:

  • Full Name and Profile Picture
  • Email Address (used for authentication & profile matching)
  • User Unique Identifier (Google UID)

We do not access, request, or store your private contacts, Google Drive files, or Gmail messages.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your profile data is strictly utilized to:

  • Authenticate you into your Chapter Director Portal securely.
  • Associate your profile with your chapter organization.
  • Display your profile details on chapter management screens.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Data is processed securely via Google Firebase Authentication and Supabase infrastructure under industry-standard TLS encryption.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You may request complete removal of your profile and data at any time by contacting our support team at connect@handysolver.com. Upon request, all user tokens and data will be permanently purged within 30 days.

6. Security

All transmissions take place over HTTPS (256-bit SSL). Credentials and OAuth tokens are handled directly by Google authentication servers; raw user passwords are never received or stored on our servers.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at connect@handysolver.com.
